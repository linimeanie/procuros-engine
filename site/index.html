Four steps from signal to tier

Every fund is scored on the same formula, so the ranking can be checked and argued with.

1 · Scale

Each signal becomes 0 to 1

best fund = 1, worst = 0

Counts are log-scaled first, so one 16-brand portfolio doesn't flatten everyone else.

2 · Pillars

Signals add up into four pillars

pillar = Σ weight × signal

Fit, pain, access and strategic value. The weights inside each pillar add up to 100%.

3 · Score

Pillars add up into one score

Access and strategic value scale down for funds with fewer than 3 target brands: reach is worth little without brands to reach.

4 · Tier

The score sets the tier

A: 38+ and 3+ brands · B: 30+

Both lines sit in natural gaps between scores. The Ranking tab shows every fund on that scale.

When a brand shows more than one signal

Most signals describe brand pain. Two rules decide how several of them combine: older news counts for less, and signals that land close together count for more, because that overlap is when a brand is most likely to buy.

Rule 1 · Recency

News loses half its weight every 9 months

A story from this month counts in full, one from 18 months ago counts a quarter, and anything older than 24 months isn't counted. Open job ads are live, so they don't decay.

Rule 2 · Stacking

Triggers in the same 12 months multiply

Four kinds of trigger count: a new listing, a funding round, ops hiring and export news. Each extra one adds 25% to the brand's pain, capped at 1.5×.

Known gaps

Most small UK companies don't publish turnover, so size comes from the accounts type filed at Companies House, with retail reach as the fallback. A missing retailer means none was confirmed, not that the brand isn't stocked. Job boards add little: these brands mostly hire through LinkedIn and recruiters.
